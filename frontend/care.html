How was this decided?

The system learned how this turbine normally behaves from its own past data.

When readings stay away from that normal pattern for about 12 hours in total, it marks the turbine Inspect now. After about 4 hours it shows Keep an eye on.

It does not confirm a fault or predict when equipment will fail.
