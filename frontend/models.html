How the models fit together

InputTurbine data10-minute averages, 42 to about 220 sensors
→
Step 1 · DetectCARE autoencoderIs this turbine behaving unusually, and which sensors are furthest from normal?
→
Step 2 · Diagnose (optional)CWRU bearing classifierOnly if a vibration recording is taken at the flagged bearing. Names the fault type.

The autoencoder does not classify faults. Its "check first" hint ranks sensors by deviation and maps them to display-only component groups. The classifier needs 48 kHz vibration recordings, which the turbine data does not contain, so the two are not connected automatically.
