How it works

Turbines already record hundreds of readings every ten minutes. Early Warning turns them into a short list of what to check.

  1. 1Learns what normal looks likeFor each turbine, a model studies a year of its own readings from when nothing was wrong.
  2. 2Notices when it driftsIf readings stay away from that normal pattern for about 12 hours, the turbine is marked Inspect now.
  3. 3Points to where to startIt lists the sensors furthest from normal, in plain words. An engineer decides what to do and records why.

About the project

A Machine Learning–Based Early Fault Warning System for Renewable Energy Equipment, a final-year ICS Project II.

Wind turbines are the main case study. A bearing fault classifier and a hydro plant study are kept as supporting research.
